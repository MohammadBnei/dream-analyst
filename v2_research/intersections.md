## Why this document exists

A decision on one plane forces a decision on another. The edges are where the work is; this file keeps them from being duplicated across three plane documents or dropped between them.

---

## P1 × P2 — every UI promise costs a data model

**Stated, not yet worked.** The narration model makes promises that are storage and modelling decisions in disguise:

- « Nothing is lost » — the historique, reversible, with a restore point before each destructive operation.
- The *portée* of a symbole — beat, scène or rêve, changeable after the fact, with inferred occurrences marked *déduit* and removable.
- The diff — additive changes applied silently, modifications proposed with every affected location shown before anything is overwritten.
- The counts — a symbole carries a number across the dreamer's entire history, not just this dream.

None of these have been costed.

## P2 × P3 — the dream as the REPL variable

**Hypothesis, flagged in the first session.** If the rêve is the variable the agent explores, then the agent does not *receive* the dream, it *explores* it — and the event stream and the agent's code may be one object seen twice rather than two systems to integrate.

This is the most promising and least examined edge. Nothing here is decided.

---

## P3 × P1 — every autonomous decision must be refusable

**Partly decided on P1, uncosted on P3.** The agent classifies dimension and operation on its own, proposes modifications, opens fils, infers symboles and marks them *déduit*. The narration model already answers the UX half: nothing destructive applies without consent, and everything inferred is refusable.

**The open tension.** P3's ambitions and P1's latency budget pull in opposite directions. An RLM exploring 100+ dreams is slow and expensive; the narration loop must feel instant, and the fixed premise says the dreamer must never wait. This suggests — does not establish — that the agent editing the dream while you speak and the agent that knows your whole history are different animals meeting at defined points. **Open. Enki's call.**
