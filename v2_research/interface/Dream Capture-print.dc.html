Phase de narration · le modèle arrêté

The dream is a document the agent edits while you talk

Not a transcript. Every chunk you speak lands as a change you can see — a beat appended, a scene cut, a symbol attached, a contradiction flagged — and every change can be walked back. Eight rules hold across every screen below.

1 · One entry. Voice or text, same composer, same pipeline. STT writes into the input zone and you send explicitly, so nothing enters the dream misheard. 4a, 5b
2 · Beats inside scenes. The beat is the unit, the scene is the container, hard cuts are objects, gaps and uncertain order are shown rather than resolved. 3a, 3c
3 · Three dimensions. Narration is bare prose and carries the dream. Vu is generated imagery in the margin. Symboles is the only accent-tinted thing — five categories, plus beat notes for point de vue and doute. They interleave in one chronological margin, not three columns. 9a, 12a
4 · Symbols carry a scope. A beat, a scene, or the whole dream — visible as reach, widened by speaking. Beat by default, promoted later. Inferred occurrences are marked déduit and refusable. 6a, 9b
5 · The agent chooses the diff. It classifies dimension and operation itself. Additive changes land silently; every modification is proposed before it overwrites. 4a, 1c
6 · Cliquer vise, parler modifie. A finger may only aim, navigate or confirm. There are no edit buttons anywhere — reordering, rescoping, regenerating are all said. 7a, 7c
7 · Everything conversational is an anchored thread. The agent's questions, your questions and the later exploration all hang off a beat, scene, symbol or the dream. None block, none expire, and a thread can still produce a diff. 11a, 8a
8 · Nothing is lost. Every operation sits in an historique you can reverse, and « je ne sais plus » is a real answer that becomes a doute. 5c, 8a

Toutes les maquettes utilisent le même rêve — hôpital / boîte de nuit / océan, 3 scènes, 25 beats — et le même vocabulaire. Nocturne · fond sombre, un seul accent en trait et en halo · Inter · images générées en placeholder.

10Le motif de réponse · court et long
Intent becomes the agent's third classification, alongside dimension and operation. A question produces no diff, so a reply must be legible as not a change: no accent bar, no apply/refuse, nothing entering the record. Two lengths, because "combien de fois l'eau ?" and "pourquoi cette femme revient ?" are different objects.
10aRéponse courte. Sits above the composer, dismisses on your next utterance. No container, no accent, no apply — a diff is a card with buttons, so plain text reads as "nothing changed" without saying it.
